Terms of use

Conditions for this personal analytics application and its informational website.

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Personal use

NT YouTube Analytics is an owner-operated application for analysing the operator’s own authorised YouTube channels. It is not a paid service and does not offer public account registration. Use requires authority to access the selected channel and compliance with the applicable Google and YouTube terms.

Read-only operation

The YouTube authorisation is used to retrieve channel data and reports. It does not permit this application to upload, modify or delete videos through the requested read-only scopes. Access may be revoked at any time in Google Account settings.

Reports and availability

Analytics may be delayed, incomplete, revised or unavailable because of provider reporting schedules, permissions, quotas or technical failures. Reports support the operator’s judgement and are not a guarantee of channel growth, revenue or future performance.

Third-party services

The application relies on Google APIs and owner-authorised storage and analysis services. Their applicable terms and policies continue to govern those services. NT YouTube Analytics is not endorsed by or affiliated with Google, YouTube or GitHub.

Privacy and contact

Read the privacy policy for data access, storage, retention and revocation details. Contact the operator through the support address shown on the OAuth consent screen, or use the public website issue tracker for general questions without private information.
