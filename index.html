Channel insights, privately.

NT YouTube Analytics is a personal application for reading and analysing the operator’s own YouTube channel data.

01

Read-only access

The application uses YouTube Data, YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting APIs to retrieve authorised channel information and aggregate performance reports. It does not upload, edit or delete videos.

02

Owner-controlled analysis

Views, watch time, engagement, audience retention, traffic sources and available impressions and click-through metrics support longitudinal channel analysis. Access is limited to channels authorised by their owner.

03

Private records

Reports are stored in the operator’s private Google Drive and Google Sheets. This website publishes information about the application; it does not publish channel analytics or collect Google credentials.

Personal application

NT YouTube Analytics supports analysis of the owner-controlled @NikolaTangalov channel. It is not a public sign-up service and offers no paid subscriptions.
