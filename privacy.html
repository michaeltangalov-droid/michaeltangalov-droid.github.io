Privacy policy

How NT YouTube Analytics handles authorised channel information.

Scope and operator

This policy covers the personal NT YouTube Analytics application operated by the owner of the linked YouTube channel, and this informational website. Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Information accessed

With the owner’s Google consent, the application requests the youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly permissions. It reads channel and video metadata and aggregate reporting data, including views, watch time, engagement, retention, traffic sources and available impressions and click-through metrics. Aggregate audience reports do not identify individual viewers. The application does not request access to Gmail, contacts or payment information through this YouTube authorisation.

Purpose and storage

Data is used for the operator’s channel performance analysis, comparisons and reporting. OAuth credentials and tokens are held in owner-controlled Google Apps Script property stores. Reports and working records are kept in private Google Drive and Google Sheets. Owner-authorised workflows may use ChatGPT and Composio to retrieve or analyse relevant records under their respective service terms and account settings.

Sharing and use restrictions

The application does not sell channel information, use it for advertising or publish private analytics on this website. Google user data is not used by this application to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. Access is limited to the operator and services the operator authorises for the stated analysis purpose.

The application’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Historical reports are retained in owner-controlled storage for longitudinal analysis and audit. The operator can delete stored reports, revoke connected-service access and remove stored OAuth tokens. Revoking access stops future retrieval; it does not automatically erase previously stored reports. These must be removed separately from the operator’s records and any connected service copies.

Google access can be reviewed or revoked through Google Account connections. The application offers read-only YouTube access; revocation does not delete YouTube videos.

This website

This static site has no application analytics scripts, advertising, forms or sign-in controls. It does not ask visitors for credentials or collect OAuth tokens. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process standard request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links are subject to their providers’ policies.

Contact

Contact the operator using the user support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen. General questions about this website may also be submitted through its public issue tracker. Do not post credentials or private analytics there.
